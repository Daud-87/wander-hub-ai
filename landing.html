About the Project

Wander Hub Ai is a Final Year Project developed to address real-world travel management challenges by integrating modern web technologies with artificial intelligence. The platform provides an intelligent and user-friendly solution for travelers, guides, and administrators through role-based dashboards.

The system focuses on smart automation, secure authentication, intelligent recommendations, and real-time communication while promoting local tourism in Pakistan. Wander Hub Ai aims to enhance travel experiences by making planning, communication, and decision-making more efficient and accessible.
